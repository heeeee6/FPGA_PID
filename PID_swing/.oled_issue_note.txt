Since pid_swing_top passes this rst_n directly to oled_ssd1306 and i2c_txn_master, low/disconnected/miswired J6-9 keeps OLED in reset forever. Proposed patch: decouple OLED reset. Generate por_rst_n from clock/config independent of external rst_n (counter should not clear on rst_n low), use control_rst_n/ctrl_run_enable only for control/motor. Add separate oled_rst_n port through pid_swing_top or instantiate OLED wrapper separately; connect OLED to por_rst_n (or an internal OLED reset that ignores J6-9), while rst_n and run_enable gate control/motor. Keep motor safe inhibit. Add sim with rst_n low/run_enable low confirming OLED I2C toggles after startup. Hardware check J6-9/J6-10 ~3.3V, SCL/SDA pullups.
